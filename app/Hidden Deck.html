♟ OLYMPUS CHESS FUSION

Hidden Deck Stat Battle

How a round works: choose one attribute → lock it → draw both hidden cards → compare only that attribute → higher value wins the round.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Both decks stay hidden. There is no visible hand.
  2. Before cards appear, choose Raw, Defense, Speed, Tactics, or Magic.
  3. Press LOCK & DRAW. Your choice cannot change after this point.
  4. Both cards flip at the same time.
  5. Only the chosen attribute is compared. Higher value wins that round.
  6. Equal final values are a draw.
  7. Simple fusion abilities may modify the chosen stat when their condition applies.
  8. First player to 5 round wins takes the match. If decks run out first, highest score wins.
