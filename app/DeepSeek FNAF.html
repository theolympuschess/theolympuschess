HOW TO PLAY

GOAL

  • Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM without letting the chess pals get you or running out of power.

CONTROLS

  • Mouse / drag: look left and right around the office.
  • A / D: close or open the left / right door.
  • Q / E: left / right flashlight.
  • Space: raise or lower the camera monitor.
  • You can also click the red and grey wall buttons beside each door, or use the on-screen buttons.
  • Doors and lights cannot be used while the monitor is up.

CAMERA

  • Raise the monitor and tap a CAM button on the map to watch a room.
  • Track Rook (left side), Knight and Queen (right side) as they move toward your doors.
  • Bishop hides in his cove (CAM 1C). Keep an eye on it, because when it empties he sprints down the left hall.

DOORS AND FLASHLIGHTS

  • Use a flashlight to check the doorway. A shadow means a pal is waiting outside.
  • Close the door while someone is there. They will wander off after a few seconds.
  • Do not leave a pal waiting at an open door, or they will attack.
  • If you hear running, close the left door fast. Bishop bumps it and costs you a little power, but if it is open he gets in.

MASK

  • If something enters the office (spooky music, flickering lights), press MASK immediately.
  • Keep it on until the piece leaves. You only have about 1.5 seconds to react.
  • Wearing the mask turns your flashlights off and lowers the monitor.

POWER

  • Doors, lights and the monitor all drain power. The bars at the top right show your usage.
  • If power hits 0%, everything shuts down and you are in danger.
  • Pals get bolder from 2 AM to 4 AM, so save power early.

WIND UP CHARGER

  • The charger slowly runs down all the time. If it reaches 0%, the power goes out.
  • Raise the monitor, then click and hold the Wind Up Charger box to refill it.
  • Winding only works while the monitor is up. It drains again when you lower the monitor.
  • A yellow caution sign appears above the monitor button when the charger drops below 20%.
  • A blinking red caution sign appears when it drops below 10%. Raise the monitor and wind it up right away!
  • The signs also show while you are wearing the mask.

OTHER BUTTONS

  • PAUSE: freezes the night. LOGS: shows or hides the event messages. HOME: returns to the main menu.
