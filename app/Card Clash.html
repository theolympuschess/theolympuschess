HOW TO PLAY

  1. Each battle begins when the leader plays one chess card.
  2. The first card sets the required color: WHITE or BLACK.
  3. The responder must follow that color if they have it.
  4. When both cards match the lead color, higher power wins. Pawn 1, Knight/Bishop 3, Rook 5, Queen 9.
  5. If you cannot follow color, you may discard another color, but it cannot defeat the lead card.
  6. Equal power triggers WAR. More cards enter the pot until somebody wins.
  7. The winner captures the whole pot and leads the next battle.
  8. When no playable cards remain, whoever captured more cards wins the match.

Example: Black Pawn (1) leads. Black Rook (5) wins. But White Queen (9) cannot beat it when Black is required.
