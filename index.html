Frequently Asked Questions

▸ What is the ideal age to start learning at Olympus Chess Academy?

We welcome learners as young as 5 years old. There’s no upper age limit — passion and curiosity are the only prerequisites at Olympus.

▸ Do I need to know chess basics before enrolling? 

Not at all! Olympus Chess Academy offers beginner-friendly modules for newcomers, as well as advanced programs for seasoned players.

▸ What is the student-to-coach ratio at Olympus?

We maintain an optimal max 6:1 ratio in group classes, ensuring personalized attention to every learner at Olympus.

▸ How frequently are the classes held?

Our regular classes at Olympus run twice a week. We also offer intensive weekend Masterclass and holiday camps.

▸ Are both group and one-on-one sessions available?

Absolutely. Olympus Chess Academy provides flexible options — structured group learning or dedicated one-on-one mentorship.

▾ What is the fee structure at Olympus Chess Academy?

We follow a transparent and accessible pricing policy. Detailed plans, including monthly and term packages, are listed under the Programs & Pricing section on our official Olympus website.

▸ What happens if a student misses a class?

We offer a reschedule credit for all missed classes if the disruption comes from Olympus. 

▸ How can a student achieve a FIDE rating through Olympus?

Our structured 6-month FIDE roadmap includes tournament preparation, official participation guidance, and rating performance analysis — all under the Olympus mentorship.

▸ What does the curriculum at Olympus include?

The Olympus Chess Academy curriculum spans tactics, strategy, openings, endgames, blindfold training, and real-game analysis — designed to foster FIDE-level excellence. Check Olympus Program

▸ Does my child need to know chess before joining Olympus Chess Academy?

Not at all! Olympus welcomes complete beginners. We have a specially designed foundational course that introduces the game in a fun and structured way.

▸ Will my child have opportunities to play in real tournaments?

Yes! At Olympus Chess Academy, we actively prepare and guide students to participate in FIDE-rated events, school tournaments, and online competitions.

▸ Are there any learning materials or resources provided?

Absolutely. We provide curated PDFs, tactical puzzles, game analysis videos, and regular assignments — all crafted in-house by Olympus mentors.

▸ How can I track my child's progress?

We maintain detailed student profiles at Olympus. Parents receive  progress reports, skill evaluations, and personalized improvement notes.

▸ Can my child learn at a comfortable pace?

Yes. Olympus Chess Academy follows a student-centric approach. Our classes are tailored to each learner’s pace, ensuring understanding and confidence before progression.

▸ What will my child learn in the first few classes?

In the first few sessions, students learn the rules of chess, basic tactics, and opening principles — all presented through engaging methods at Olympus.

▸ Will my child receive feedback on games and mistakes?

Definitely. Regular game reviews and one-on-one feedback sessions are an integral part of training at Olympus Chess Academy. We help each child understand not just what they played, but why certain moves work better.
